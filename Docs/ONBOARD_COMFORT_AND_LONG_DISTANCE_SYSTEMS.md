# Onboard comfort and long-distance service systems

## Product requirement

The coach must feel like a real long-distance passenger vehicle. The player is responsible for driving safely and operating onboard systems. These systems must affect passengers, operating costs, energy use, maintenance, and company reputation where appropriate.

## Driver-operated equipment

- Air conditioning: power, temperature setting, fan speed, ventilation mode, passenger comfort, and fuel/energy impact.
- Radio/audio: power, volume, station selection, mute, and driver-safe controls.
- Passenger television: power, screen status, entertainment availability, and passenger satisfaction.
- Public-address microphone: route announcements, safety instructions, station announcements, and stop notifications.
- Interior lighting: main cabin lights, aisle lights, reading lights, and night dimming.
- Dashboard and instrument illumination.
- Windscreen wipers and washer fluid, headlights, high beams, hazard lights, and fog lights.
- Doors and luggage-compartment doors, with safety checks before moving.
- USB/power outlets and passenger Wi-Fi, where fitted.

## Passenger safety and comfort

- Seat belts: visible belt state where supported, pre-departure reminders, and safety compliance.
- Reclining seats, footrests, curtains, reading lights, individual air vents, and charging ports as coach equipment options.
- Passenger boarding and seat assignment; passenger count must not exceed legal vehicle capacity.
- Luggage loading, storage capacity, luggage checks, and unloading at destinations.
- Accessibility support and priority seating.
- Passenger complaints and requests, including temperature, volume, cleanliness, and comfort.
- Emergency exits, fire extinguisher, first-aid kit, and safety briefing.
- Cabin cleanliness, waste collection, and cleaning between trips.

## Food and onboard services

- Stock and sell or distribute bottled water, soft drinks, snacks, and meals.
- Inventory, purchase costs, sale prices, stock expiry/availability, and service revenue.
- Passenger requests and service interactions.
- Optional onboard attendant/host recruitment for larger company operations.
- Food and drink service must not distract the driver while the coach is moving.

## Long-distance journey management

- Scheduled rest stops, fuel stops, meal breaks, and passenger toilet breaks.
- Route timetable, estimated arrival time, delays, traffic, tolls, and road incidents.
- Passenger comfort over time, including fatigue, temperature, cleanliness, and entertainment.
- Optional coaches with onboard toilet; toilet availability, cleaning, water supply, and maintenance.
- Driver fatigue and mandatory rest periods as gameplay systems, designed to encourage safe operation.
- Trip preparation checklist: vehicle condition, fuel, tyres, lights, doors, luggage, passenger count, safety equipment, and onboard supplies.

## Gameplay and interface requirements

- Provide an interactive cockpit control panel for the driver.
- Provide a separate passenger-service/attendant interface so the driver is not expected to operate every service while driving.
- Controls must be large, reachable, and safe on Android touchscreens.
- Use context-sensitive prompts when parked or at a station for detailed interactions.
- Important safety controls must never be hidden behind decorative menus.
- Equipment availability depends on the selected coach model and its installed options.

## Simulation rules

- Equipment consumes power and may affect fuel use, battery load, maintenance, and operating expenses.
- Incorrect operation can reduce passenger satisfaction or create safety penalties.
- Correct service improves comfort, ratings, repeat customers, and company reputation.
- The player must be able to inspect and configure the coach before departure.

## Development priority

1. Basic interactive climate, cabin lights, radio, and passenger announcement controls.
2. Passenger comfort feedback and seat-belt safety checks.
3. Luggage and boarding workflow.
4. Food/drink inventory and sales.
5. TV, Wi-Fi, USB, reading lights, curtains, and seat options.
6. Toilet-equipped coach variants, attendant role, cleaning, and deeper long-haul operations.

These are approved product requirements, not claims that the systems are already implemented.
