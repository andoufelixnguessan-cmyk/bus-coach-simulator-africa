# Passenger door and safety interlock

The driving prototype now has a dedicated passenger entrance door.

- The mobile landscape HUD displays **PORTE FERMÉE / PORTE OUVERTE** and toggles the door.
- The vehicle controller rejects door operation above 1 km/h.
- The generated door panel animates between closed and open positions.
- The visual door is attached to the prototype coach at runtime.
- The door panel is a prototype visual and is not yet a production-quality model or a fully simulated pneumatic mechanism.

This feature has been structurally checked in the repository. It has not yet been compiled or tested in Unity on an Android device.
